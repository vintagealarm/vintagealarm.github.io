## 3. Critical boundary — no silent merger with H.M.C. or Hirota

No inspected result in this run exposes `H.M.C.`, `Hirota`, `廣田`, a company address, trademark proprietor, patent applicant, warranty entity, box label, or distributor on the same `Modern De Luxe` lighter.

Therefore it is NOT yet established that:

- these Modern De Luxe lighters were made or sold by H.M.C.;
- H.M.C. = Hirota;
- Hirota operated the Modern De Luxe brand;
- the Modern De Luxe lighter and clock lines had the same operator;
- a shared brand string alone proves common origin.

The strongest alternative explanation remains live: `Modern De Luxe` may have been a separate or generic trade designation used by another Japanese exporter/manufacturer.
