## 3. Official Hirota chronology remains compatible but does not prove the expansion

Lightec official history independently confirms the Hirota corporate sequence and business context:
- 1937 founding;
- 1946 move to Asakusabashi;
- 1947 `株式会社廣田製作所`;
- 1953 Shiseido Hanatsubaki-kai commemorative-goods orders;
- 1954 `株式会社広田`;
- 1963 `モダンダイカスト工場株式会社`;
- 1964 export-contributing-company recognition;
- 1971 `モダン化学工場株式会社` and Modern Royal sales strengthening.

Source:
- https://lightec-inc.jp/corporate/history.html

This chronology makes a Hirota MODERN/H.M.C. relationship historically plausible, especially in promotional goods, but it does not expand the initials H.M.C.
