Date: 2026-10-03 JST

Result: NO MATERIAL PROGRESS / PLATFORM NOT YET IDENTIFIED

## Current state

A base Modern De Luxe movement/platform cannot yet be named.

Known exact-brand evidence now includes both 2-jewel and 5-jewel mechanical clocks. That is not enough to decide whether they are:
- jewel-count variants of one platform;
- related movements from one maker;
- or different sourced movements sold under the same clock brand.

## Why jewel count cannot solve the platform

Period comparison products show that one clock maker could sell different jewel-count specifications within its clock ranges. Current Rhythm records, for example, preserve Japan-marked alarm clocks described with 2-, 5-, and 8-jewel specifications.

Therefore the new Modern De Luxe 5-jewel example does not, by itself, prove a second maker or a different platform.

Likewise, the widespread Japanese 2-jewel alarm category means the 2-jewel marking cannot identify the maker.

## Platform gate

This VA box becomes actionable when at least one of the following is recovered:
- exposed Modern De Luxe movement;
- maker or caliber mark;
- movement dimensions;
- a maker-marked comparator with matching plate/control architecture;
- service/parts documentation naming the caliber.

Until then, do not invent a caliber family and do not use jewel count as a proxy.

## Closing rule

Box ⑤ stays OPEN and bounded.

This does not block the DEEP DIVE. If the base platform remains unknown after the physical-source routes are exhausted, the finished research should state that directly.
