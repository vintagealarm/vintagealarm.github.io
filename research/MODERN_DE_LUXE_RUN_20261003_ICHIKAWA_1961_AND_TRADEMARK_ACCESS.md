## 1. New primary patent evidence: Kaname Ichikawa was already active in lighter invention before the 1971 Hirota Modern Trading joint venture

Google Patents record `DE1157020B` identifies:

- inventors / applicants: **Kaname Ichikawa** and **Kunio Ikeda**, Tokyo
- Japanese priority dates/numbers:
  - 1961-02-28 — Showa 36-8893
  - 1961-05-08 — Showa 36-23009
  - 1961-05-08 — Showa 36-23010
- subject: gas lighter

Source:
https://patents.google.com/patent/DE1157020B/en

A separate U.S. patent record, `US2921495A`, identifies **Kaname Ichikawa** on a lighter invention with Japanese priority dated **1958-02-11**.

Source:
https://patents.google.com/patent/US2921495A/en

### What this directly establishes

The Ichikawa / Royal-side lineage had independent lighter-development activity by at least 1958–1961, well before the documented 1971 Royal Sangyo × Hirota Modern Trading joint venture that created the Modern Royal name.

### What this does NOT establish

It does not show that:

- Ichikawa owned `MODERN`, `MODERN LITE`, `H.M.C.`, or `Modern De Luxe`;
- H.M.C. was Royal / Ichikawa;
- Hirota was uninvolved in the pre-1971 MODERN corpus;
- the Modern De Luxe clocks were supplied by Ichikawa / Royal.

### Research effect

This materially strengthens the alternative explanation that the pre-1971 Japanese lighter ecosystem relevant to the later Modern Royal lineage contained **more than one independently active actor**. Therefore a pre-1971 `MODERN`-family object cannot be assigned to Hirota merely from the later 1971 company combination or from the word `Modern`.

The 1961 JPO reverse-search must now keep at least two patent families separate:

- Hirota / Yoshio Hirota / Asakusabashi leads;
- Ichikawa / Ikeda gas-lighter applications.

Do not collapse them unless a contemporaneous source joins them.
