# Modern De Luxe — 1961 JPO / Hirota personnel lead — 2026-10-01

Scope: research-only evidence note for PR #135. No public-site change.

## Classification

**MATERIAL LEAD PROGRESS, NO GATE PASS.** Personnel-name reverse searching surfaced a potentially high-value 1961 Japan Patent Office document lead tying a `広田良夫` to a lighter-related record and a precise Asakusabashi address. The underlying JPO document has not yet been independently recovered/read, so the lead is not promoted to primary evidence and G3 remains OPEN/HOLD.

## 1. Locator lead

A 2021 r/translator thread was created by users who said they possessed a Japan Patent Office document for a lighter and wanted the Japanese text translated. The translation in that thread reports:

- address: `Tokyo-to, Taito-ku, Asakusabashi 3-19`;
- application/issue context: Showa 36 (1961);
- owner/name: `広田良夫`, tentatively romanized by the commenter as Yoshio Hirota;
- no company name shown in the translated document.

Source:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/translator/comments/mi6492

This is **not yet accepted as the JPO document's contents**, because the original JPO image/document has not been independently recovered and checked. Reddit is used only as a locator/index to the underlying primary record.

## 2. Independent later patent control for the name

USPTO-derived records independently show a **Yoshio Hirota (Tokyo)** as inventor of multiple cigarette-lighter designs filed in 1984–1985, with **Kabushiki Kaisha Hirota** as assignee. Example: U.S. design patent D284410, filed 1984-12-06, patented 1986-06-24.

Sources:
- https://patents.justia.com/patent/D284410
- https://patents.justia.com/inventor/yoshio-hirota

This establishes that a later lighter inventor named Yoshio Hirota was directly associated with Kabushiki Kaisha Hirota. It does **not** by itself prove that the `広田良夫` in the reported 1961 JPO document is the same individual.

## 3. Address-control result — 2026-10-01 follow-up

The address path was tested independently rather than assuming that any Asakusabashi address proves identity.

Lightec's current official company profile gives its headquarters as:

- `東京都台東区浅草橋2-13-9`
- company origin: 1937; incorporation: 1954.

Source:
- https://lightec-inc.jp/corporate/

An independent Thai Hirota company profile likewise names Lightec Inc. as the parent company and gives the same Tokyo parent address, `2-13-9 Asakusabashi, Taito-ku`, while identifying Shozo Hirota as Thai Hirota president and the business as manufacture of cigarette lighters and related products.

Source:
- https://www.wesleynet.com/thailand/ecatalogue/profile.php?Co_Code=1105

**Control result:** the reported 1961 address `Asakusabashi 3-19` is **not a match** to the currently documented Lightec/Hirota address `Asakusabashi 2-13-9`. No inference of continuity, renumbering, relocation, or transcription error is made. Historical address evidence is required.

Current web results also show modern unrelated businesses at `Asakusabashi 3-19-*`; these are noise and are not evidence about the 1961 record.

## 4. Name-search collision control

Searching `広田良夫 / 廣田良夫 / Yoshio Hirota` produces substantial unrelated-name noise, including later academic authors. Therefore name-only matches are insufficient. A valid bridge must carry at least one additional discriminator such as lighter subject matter, Hirota company affiliation, historical address, application number, or contemporaneous corporate record.

This strengthens the requirement not to equate the 1961 `広田良夫` lead with the 1984–85 Kabushiki Kaisha Hirota inventor on romanized-name similarity alone.

## 6. Why the lead still matters

If the original 1961 JPO record is recovered and confirms the reported name/address, it could provide a period person/address fingerprint decades earlier than the 1983 UNIDO record. That fingerprint can then be tested against:

- historical Hirota corporate addresses/directories;
- `H.M.C.CO.` packaging or catalogue addresses;
- MODERN / Modernlite trademark records;
- the 1966 `広苑 第3号` article on the Asakusabashi lighter industry.

A direct period address/person match between Hirota and H.M.C./MODERN would materially advance G3. No such match has yet been recovered.
