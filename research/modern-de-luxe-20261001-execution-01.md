## Results and evidence status

### 1. 1961 JPO locator remains unpromoted
The surviving Reddit translator thread states that the uploader had obtained a Japan Patent Office record for the lighter and that another user read it as Showa 36 (1961), owner `広田良夫`, address `東京都台東区浅草橋3-19`, with no company name. The original JPO image/publication number was not recovered in this pass.

Status: LOCATOR ONLY. Do not cite as verified JPO content until the original record is recovered.

### 2. Independent Hirota export/manufacturing chain remains strong but does not solve H.M.C.
The 1976 U.S. customs case records a late-November 1970 order for 500,000 HD-600 disposable lighters placed with manufacturer `K. K. Hirota, Tokyo, Japan`. A 1975 affidavit identifies Sonosuke Aoyagi as vice president of Hirota Company, employed there for 18 years and responsible for sales and financing. This independently supports Hirota as an exporter/manufacturer before the 1971 Modern Royal event, but contains no H.M.C., MODERN or Modern De Luxe bridge.

### 3. Later Hirota designer/company chain remains independently documented
U.S. design patent D284410: inventor Yoshio Hirota (Tokyo), assignee Kabushiki Kaisha Hirota (Tokyo), filed 1984-12-06. Later patent 6,287,109 identifies Ryohei Hirota (Tokyo), assignee Hirota & Co., Ltd. (Tokyo). These establish later Hirota lighter-design identities but do not by themselves prove that the 1961 `広田良夫` locator is the same Yoshio Hirota.

### 4. Modern Royal / Ichikawa chain remains separate and explicit
Modern Royal-assigned U.S. lighter designs identify Kaname Ichikawa as inventor from at least the 1976 filing represented by D246996 onward. This is useful as a control: the Modern Royal line can be documented explicitly when the assignee is known. No comparable retrieved patent in this pass explicitly assigns H.M.C. or Modern De Luxe to Hirota.

### 5. H.M.C. corpus remains real but legally unidentified
Current marketplace/searchable records still expose `MODERN H.M.C.co` and `Modern H.M.C / Modernlite Super Lighter Art No 510` specimens. No newly retrieved source supplies an H.M.C. address, expanded legal name, proprietor, trademark owner, or direct Hirota attribution from a contemporaneous authoritative source.

### 6. Modern De Luxe lighter corpus remains real but does not yield the bridge
AAA Southern California and Cott Soda Modern De Luxe advertising lighters remain searchable and Japan-attributed by sellers. No newly retrieved high-resolution bottom/box/guarantee evidence yielded H.M.C., Hirota, an address, or a manufacturer/distributor name.
