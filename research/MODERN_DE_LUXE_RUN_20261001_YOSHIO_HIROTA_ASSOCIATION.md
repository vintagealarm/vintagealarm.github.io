## 3. Important non-identity boundary

The above does **not** yet prove that all three records refer to the same human being, and especially does not prove that the 1961 `広田良夫` locator is the later Hirota-company inventor / association-property representative.

Reasons to hold:

- the 1961 evidence is currently only a translated locator, not the original gazette;
- the municipal ledger does not state `株式会社広田` or a job title for 廣田良夫;
- the later patent records do not supply the 1961 Asakusabashi 3-19 address;

Therefore this is an **identity-candidate chain**, not an adopted identity finding.
