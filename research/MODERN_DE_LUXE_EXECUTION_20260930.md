## Alternative explanation test

The strongest alternative remains that `Modern De Luxe` and `Modern H.M.C./Modernlite` were separate Japanese trade names operating in the same mid-century export/premium-gift market. The current evidence is fully compatible with that alternative. Therefore similarity of market role cannot be promoted to common ownership.

The generic nature of the phrase `Modern De Luxe` also produces unrelated-product false positives; unrelated sewing machines and other products are excluded unless a mark/logo/company chain independently connects them.
