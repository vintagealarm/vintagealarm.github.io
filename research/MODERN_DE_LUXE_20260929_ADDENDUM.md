# Modern De Luxe — 2026-09-29/30 research addendum

This addendum records evidence found after the current `MODERN_DE_LUXE_LEDGER.md` update. It does not change public WATCH prose or claim a final manufacturer attribution. Merge into the main ledger after the evidence set stabilizes.

## 1. Modern De Luxe Mitsubishi Nagoya 50th-anniversary travel alarm — HOLD, strong dating anchor

A surviving Japanese shop/archive page documents a mechanical `MODERN DE LUXE / ALARM CLOCK` travel alarm, metal/gold-colour, coin design, approx. 6.7 cm diameter and 3.5 cm depth. The item bears the inscription `三菱電機株式会社 名古屋製作所50周年記念`.

Source:
https://noraneco1972.jimdofree.com/ブログ-menu/時計/トラベルクロック/

The seller/page dates the piece to 1974 from the Nagoya Works' 1924 establishment and describes it as a likely commemorative novelty. The same page states that the clock and alarm use a single mainspring and that the alarm functions.

**Directly supported by the page:**
- a surviving `MODERN DE LUXE / ALARM CLOCK` travel alarm exists with a Mitsubishi Electric Nagoya Works 50th-anniversary inscription;
- it is a mechanical alarm clock and the page describes a single-mainspring clock/alarm arrangement.

**Dating judgment / HOLD:** the 50th-anniversary inscription provides a materially stronger dating anchor than a marketplace style estimate. If the anniversary is counted from the Nagoya Works' 1924 establishment, the intended anniversary year is 1974. However, this research pass has not yet obtained Mitsubishi primary documentation proving that this exact clock was distributed in 1974. Therefore record it as `1974 inferred from commemorative inscription`, not as an independently primary-source-confirmed manufacture date.

**Research significance:** this is consistent with `Modern De Luxe` product use continuing after the 1971 formation of Modern Royal. It does not by itself prove that Modern Royal, Hirota, or Hirota Modern Trading manufactured or supplied the clock.

## 6. Modern Royal official lineage remains the chronological control

Modern Royal's official detailed history states:
- 1946: Kaname Ichikawa founded export trading company Ichikawa Sangyo;
- 1965: Royal Industries Co., Ltd. established and began cigarette-lighter exports;
- 1971: joint venture with Hirota Modern Trading Co., Ltd.; company renamed Modern Royal Co., Ltd.

Source:
https://www.modernroyal.com/docs/enkaku.pdf

Therefore any product explicitly carrying `Modern Royal` branding cannot be dated to the 1960s merely from a seller estimate without explaining the conflict with the official 1971 company-name chronology.

Sources:

**Directly supported:** a Modern De Luxe travel alarm and a Tokyo Clock/Tokyo Tokei `2 Jewels` travel alarm have appeared together in a Japanese auction lot, and Tokyo Clock `2 Jewels` travel alarms form a surviving comparison corpus.

**Not supported:** the mixed lot does not establish common manufacture, shared movement, or OEM relationship. It is useful only because it identifies a contemporaneous Japanese 2-jewel travel-alarm family worth image-level topology comparison.

**Next test:** recover the lot images or independent high-resolution Tokyo Clock 2-jewel examples and compare dial diameter, rear winding/setting arbor positions, plate outline, balance position, jewel placement, alarm hammer/gong geometry, and case hinge/stand construction against Modern De Luxe 2-jewel examples.

## Current working model after this pass

Most useful model remains a separated supply-chain model rather than `Hirota made everything`:

`MODERN-family branding / export-distribution channel`
+
`one or more external clock/watch movement and case suppliers`
→
`Modern / Modern De Luxe clock products`

The 1974-inferred Mitsubishi commemorative piece strengthens continuity of `Modern De Luxe` into the post-1971 period, but does **not** yet identify the contracting/supplying company.

The 2026-09-30 comparison search adds **Tokyo Clock/Tokyo Tokei 2-jewel travel alarms as a concrete topology-comparison target**, but does not elevate Tokyo Tokei to an OEM candidate without image/mechanical matching.
