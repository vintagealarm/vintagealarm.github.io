## Control

Mandatory startup was re-run from `main/PROJECT.md` → `AGENTS.md` → `PROJECT_STATE.md` → PR #135 → latest H.M.C.CO/Hirota locator run.

This run did not accept the Mercari seller narrative as proof. It attempted to corroborate, independently and object-by-object, the three separate propositions embedded in that narrative:

1. `MODERN` was actually used on Hirota lighter products;
2. `H.M.C.CO.` identifies Hirota;
3. Modern De Luxe belongs to the same Hirota-controlled brand family.

Only proposition 1 materially advanced. Propositions 2–3 remain open.

## 1. Independent visual/documentary locator: MODERN product attributed to Hirota & Co., Ltd.

Wikimedia Commons preserves two high-resolution photographs transferred from Joe Haupt's Flickr stream under the title:

`Vintage "Derringer Gun" Table Gas Lighter by Modern, Hirota & Co., Ltd., Made in Japan`

Sources:
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vintage_%22Derringer_Gun%22_Table_Gas_Lighter_by_Modern,_Hirota_%26_Co.,_Ltd.,_Made_in_Japan_(8510784205).jpg
- https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vintage_%22Derringer_Gun%22_Table_Gas_Lighter_by_Modern,_Hirota_%26_Co.,_Ltd.,_Made_in_Japan_(8510797191).jpg
- original Flickr record: https://www.flickr.com/photos/51764518@N02/8510784205/

The Commons records preserve the original Flickr title/description and source metadata, and the images are available at 3331×2200 and 4404×2267 respectively. This is independent of the Mercari seller.

### Evidence boundary

The Commons/Flickr title itself is collector/photographer attribution, not a contemporaneous manufacturer register. It therefore cannot by itself prove every legal-brand relationship. However, it independently documents a physical `Modern` Derringer lighter identified as `Hirota & Co., Ltd.` and is materially stronger corroboration for `MODERN ↔ Hirota` than relying on the single Mercari narrative.

A separate current eBay listing for a boxed example is titled:

`Derringer Gun Modern Table Gas Lighter Hirota & Co LTD Japan`

and its gallery/image descriptions visibly describe a red/black box labelled `Modern` and `Table Gas Lighter Derringer Gun`.

Source:
- https://www.ebay.com/itm/256911927343

Again, the `Hirota & Co LTD` portion is seller attribution unless a Hirota marking is directly read from the object/box. The useful direct object fact from the accessible listing is the `Modern` box/product branding.

## 2. MODERN Duke packaging exists, but does not independently name Hirota in accessible text

A current eBay listing for a boxed `Modern Duke` lighter exposes gallery descriptions showing packaging marked `Modern Duke`; the listing title is `Antique Modern Duke Lighter New in Box`.

Source:

This supports existence of `Modern Duke` as an actual packaged product designation. Accessible listing text does not independently expose `Hirota & Co.` on the package, so it is not used as the missing company bridge.

## 3. H.M.C.CO. form independently survives, but expansion remains unproven

Mercari's current MODERN-lighter search index independently exposes a separate item titled:

`MODERN H.M.C.co 日本製オイルライター`

Source:
- https://jp.mercari.com/search?keyword=MODERN+%E3%83%A9%E3%82%A4%E3%82%BF%E3%83%BC

This confirms that `H.M.C.co` is not merely wording invented in the sold Hirota-attributed listing. It is a recurring product/market marking form.

The sold item `m87843097008` still states `広田 ( H.M.C. CO.)` and claims the base bears brand/company engraving, but the actual base image has not been recovered/read at sufficient resolution in this environment.

Source:
- https://jp.mercari.com/item/m87843097008

Therefore `H.M.C.CO. = Hirota` remains **unproven**.

`Vintage Advertising Cigarette Lighter With Finest Tobaccos Logo, Modernlite Automatic Superlighter, Art. No. 510, Modern (H.M.C.), Made In Japan, Circa 1950s`

This is consistent with the separately cataloged L.T. Jordan Institute Art.510 record and the user-supplied Art.2505 image already in the branch. It proves the H.M.C./Modernlite product corpus exists; it does not identify the legal entity behind H.M.C.

## 5. Corporate-novelty customer existence: Nakagawa Ras Seisakusho

The sold Mercari H.M.C.CO/Hirota-attributed item says it was a novelty for `中川ラス製作所`.

A Japan Pension Service PDF independently lists `中川ラス製作所` among Osaka establishments in its historical unmatched-record establishment list.

Source:
- https://www.nenkin.go.jp/service/nenkinkiroku/torikumi/mitougou/osaka.files/JigyousyoIchiran_4121_201209.pdf

This independently establishes that the named company/workshop existed in Osaka records. It does **not** establish that the Mercari lighter was commissioned by that firm or that Hirota supplied it; those still depend on the object/listing until further documentation is found.

## 6. 1961 Yoshio Hirota route rechecked without overclaim

Searches for `広田良夫` / `廣田良夫` + lighter/patent/ignition terms still did not recover the 1961 original gazette.

The later patent trail remains strong but late:
- US design patent D284410, filed 1984: inventor `Yoshio Hirota (Tokyo)`, assignee `Kabushiki Kaisha Hirota (Tokyo)`.
- Justia inventor index lists multiple 1984–85 lighter designs by Yoshio Hirota assigned to Kabushiki Kaisha Hirota.

Sources:
- https://patents.justia.com/patent/D284410
- https://patents.justia.com/inventor/yoshio-hirota

The Reddit JPO-image locator remains the only recovered 1961 pointer and reports `広田良夫`, `浅草橋3-19`, Showa 36, no company name. Do not merge the 1961 and 1984 Yoshio Hirota identities without the original/independent bridge.

## 7. Hirota Modern Trading route

Fresh Japanese and English exact-name searches for `廣田モダントレーディング` / `広田モダントレーディング` / `Hirota Modern Trading` produced no new pre-1971 independent record in this run.

Modern Royal's own history remains the authoritative accessible record that in 1971 Royal Sangyo entered a joint venture with `（株）廣田モダントレーディング`, renamed itself Modern Royal, and moved to Asakusabashi.

Source:
- https://www.modernroyal.com/docs/enkaku.pdf

No establishment date, officers, predecessor name or H.M.C. expansion was recovered.
