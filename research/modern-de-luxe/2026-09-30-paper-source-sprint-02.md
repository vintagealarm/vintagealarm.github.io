## New source route: 全国商標社章図鑑

### ADOPTED — bibliography/access

NDL confirms two editions of 学窓社『全国商標社章図鑑』:

- 1959 edition: 489p, NDL PID 2489855, digitized, library/individual transmission eligible.
  - https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R100000039-I2489855
- 1965 revised 2nd edition: 443p, NDL PID 2505033, digitized, library/individual transmission eligible.
  - https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R100000002-I000001063984

The 1965 NDL table of contents explicitly includes a `図版・業種別目次`, making this useful not only for owner-name lookup but also for industry/category traversal.

NDL Reference Collaborative Database independently recommends the 1959 and 1965 editions as sources containing company marks / corporate emblems.
- https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000136124&page=ref_view

### Why this matters

This is a separate route from 1959『日本商標大事典』. The latter is valuable for historical registered/renewed trademarks; 『全国商標社章図鑑』 can potentially expose the contemporary company mark / trade mark actually associated with a company, and the 1965 revised edition sits directly inside the target period.

## 1959 日本商標大事典 — scope rechecked

NDL confirms: 商標研究会編『日本商標大事典』, 1959, 1460p, NDL PID 1696300, digitized and individual-transmission eligible.
- https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/books/R100000002-I000000988884

A documented library reference case establishes that its `更新登録商標総覧` covers marks renewed from Showa 12 through Showa 31, arranged by prefecture and then rights-holder name, with registration number/date and mark figure.
- https://crd.ndl.go.jp/reference/entry/index.php?id=1000043080&page=ref_view

### Correction to search logic

Because the renewal register ends at Showa 31 (1956), it cannot by itself prove a newly created late-1950s MODERN mark. It is strongest for testing whether Hirota/廣田 already held an older mark that survived into the target period. Therefore:

- positive Hirota/MODERN hit = potentially strong pre-1959 anchor;
- no hit = NOT evidence that MODERN did not exist by 1959.
