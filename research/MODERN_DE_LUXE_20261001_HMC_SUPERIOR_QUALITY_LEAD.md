# Modern De Luxe / H.M.C. — Superior Quality lead

Date: 2026-10-01 JST
Scope: research evidence only; no public-site change.

## New material found

### 1. Salem advertising lighter — `Modern Superior Quality by H.M.C.`

A surviving Salem advertising lighter is indexed by eBay with the title `Vintage Salem Lighter Made In Japan Modern Superior Quality By H.M.C`. The indexed image descriptions show the Salem advertising body and H.M.C. marking. An older auction mirror independently preserves an original seller description of a Salem lighter as `a Modern Super Quality, by: H.M.C.`.

Sources:
- https://www.ebay.com/itm/188978325432
- https://avaluer.net/explore/4917038-vintage_cigarette_advertising_lighters___group_of_3___camel__salem__winston_

Evidence class: surviving-object marketplace evidence / archived seller description. This is not a corporate registry or primary company document.

### 2. H.M.C. is not confined to the `Modernlite Art. No. 510` wording

Previously known physical/object records establish `Modern H.M.C.` together with `MODERNLITE AUTOMATIC SUPERLIGHTER ART. No. 510`, including the L.T. Jordan Institute object JI2025000264 and the high-resolution Joe Haupt photograph on Wikimedia Commons.

The Salem evidence adds a materially different product wording: `Modern Superior/Super Quality by H.M.C.`. Therefore the H.M.C. mark appears across more than one product descriptor and should not be modeled solely as part of the fixed `Modernlite Art. 510` model name.

Sources:
- https://hub.catalogit.app/l.t.-jordan-institute-for-international-awareness/folder/entry/set-of-cigarette-case-and-lighter-with-oil-refinery-scene-in-blue-case

### 3. Additional model-family lead: Art. No. 2505

A current Japanese marketplace listing describes a `Modern Lite / Modern (H.M.C.)` Wind Proof Super Lighter, Art. No. 2505. This is seller-supplied attribution and is therefore only a locator until the object marking itself is independently read from a sufficiently clear image.

## Interpretation

Material progress: YES, but NO GATE PASS.

The useful change is not another Art.510 specimen. The Salem object indicates that `H.M.C.` was used with a broader `Modern ... Quality` product wording. This weakens a narrow hypothesis in which H.M.C. is merely a suffix embedded in the `Modernlite Art.510` model designation, and strengthens the need to test H.M.C. as a maker/exporter/brand-controller mark across a product family.

It still does **not** establish:
- what H.M.C. expands to;
- that H.M.C. = Hirota / Kabushiki Kaisha Hirota;
- that Modern De Luxe was an H.M.C. product;
- that the Salem lighter and Modern De Luxe clocks shared a supplier.
