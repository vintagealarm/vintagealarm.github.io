## 1. Presentation-box evidence surfaced

Source:
- https://m.bidbuy.co.jp/ebay/listings?bidtype=bid&category=593&orderby=endingSoonest&page=143&ppage=26

The indexed record identifies the object as a Modern De Luxe lighter set, Japan, and explicitly states that it is in a presentation box. ### Boundary

The currently indexed result does **not** expose readable box text beyond the seller title, and no H.M.C., Hirota, address, Art. No., patent, distributor, or trademark proprietor has yet been recovered from the box. Therefore this is a locator/acquisition lead, not a company bridge.

The presentation-box result is the most useful new object-access lead from this run because it may preserve information absent from bare lighter bodies. The next acquisition/search target should be the original/sold eBay item or mirrored images for the boxed fleur-de-lis set, specifically looking for:

- underside stamping;
- inside-lid or exterior box branding;
- paper insert / guarantee / instruction sheet;

- `H.M.C.` / `H.M.C.CO.`;
- `HIROTA` / `廣田`;
- exporter/distributor/address;
- patent / registration numbers.

Do not count additional front-only advertising variants as progress unless they expose one of these fingerprints.
