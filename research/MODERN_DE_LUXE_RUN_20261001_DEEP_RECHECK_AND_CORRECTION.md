## 1. Direct source recheck: *Alarm am Arm* p.317

The project-supplied PDF `Alarm Am Arm (1).pdf` was directly text-extracted and the relevant PDF page was rendered and visually inspected. Printed page 317 is PDF page 310 in this copy.

The Modern du Luxe entry states, in substance and directly in the source:
- `MODERN DU LUXE`
- gold-plated metal case, diameter 55 mm
- silvered dial, printed black Roman numerals
- Dauphine luminous hands
- pin-lever movement with 2 jewels
- the author characterizes it as essentially a table/stand alarm inserted into a wristwatch case
- the external crown at 3 o'clock is only a dummy
- all operating controls are on the back

The photograph on the same printed page was also directly inspected. It shows the front and back of the same object. The back has the alarm-clock controls; no company name, H.M.C., Hirota, exporter, address or other legal entity marking is legible in the inspected page image.

The movement index later in the same PDF lists `Modern du Luxe` with `Kal. Modern du Luxe`. This is a book index/calibre label and is **not** independently treated as a movement-manufacturer identification.

### Effect

This source strongly confirms the topology of the 55 mm wrist object and the 2-jewel pin-lever clock-derived movement. It does **not** identify the commercial operator or OEM. Therefore it cannot close G1.

## 4. Hirota company evidence: official history and promotional-goods relevance

Lightec's official company history was rechecked. It states:
- 1937: business founded in Taito, Tokyo
- 1946: moved to Asakusabashi
- 1947: reorganized as `株式会社廣田製作所`
- 1953: began receiving commemorative-goods orders from Shiseido Hanatsubaki-kai
- 1954: reorganized as `株式会社広田`
- 1964: new Asakusabashi headquarters completed and company recognized as an export-contributing enterprise
- 1971: disposable-lighter producer `モダン化学工場株式会社` established; `モダンロイヤル株式会社` established to strengthen sales

Source: https://www.lightec-inc.jp/corporate/history.html

This is significant to the **business-model plausibility** of G5: Hirota demonstrably handled commemorative/promotional orders long before the 1970s Modern De Luxe commemorative-clock examples. But this remains context only. It does not show that any Modern De Luxe clock was one of Hirota's orders.

## 5. Modern Royal / Hirota relationship rechecked from Modern Royal itself

Modern Royal's own history states:
- Royal Sangyo established in 1965 and exported cigarette lighters;
- in 1971 it entered a joint venture with `（株）廣田モダントレーディング`, changed name to `モダンロイヤル（株）`, and moved to Asakusabashi;
- in 2005 Modern Royal shares were transferred from `（株）廣田` to RIC.

Sources:
- https://www.modernroyal.com/recruit/history.pdf
- https://www.modernroyal.com/docs/enkaku.pdf

This preserves the handoff boundary: Modern Royal is not simply a timeless alternate name for Hirota. It also confirms that an entity named `廣田モダントレーディング` existed by the 1971 joint-venture event. It still does not identify H.M.C. or Modern De Luxe.

## 6. Independent later Hirota industrial evidence
