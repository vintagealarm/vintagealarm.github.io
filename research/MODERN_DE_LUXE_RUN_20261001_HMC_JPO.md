An independently indexed U.S. design patent also records `Yoshio Hirota (Tokyo)` as inventor and `Kabushiki Kaisha Hirota (Tokyo)` as assignee for a cigarette lighter filed 1984-12-06:

- US D284410
- https://patents.justia.com/patent/D284410

### What this advances

This establishes that a later Hirota-company lighter inventor named `廣田良夫 / Yoshio Hirota` is directly documented in patent records. It gives a concrete name/company fingerprint for reverse searching older records.

### What it does NOT establish

It does **not** prove that the unresolved 1961 locator mentioning `広田良夫` is the same person. It does not recover the 1961 publication number or original gazette. It does not identify H.M.C. and does not connect Modern De Luxe clocks to Hirota.

Therefore G3 remains OPEN/HOLD.
