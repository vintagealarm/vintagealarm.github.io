# Wittnauer 10WA — Research Direction

Status: active research direction for `research/10wa-round2`.

This note fixes the **scope, source order, and deep-dive factors** for the next 10WA research rounds. It does not replace `WITTNAUER_10WA_LEDGER.md` or `WITTNAUER_10WA_CH304088_AUDIT.md`.

## Core direction

The primary subject is **Wittnauer / Longines-Wittnauer and the production history of Cal.10WA**.

Do not let the research drift into a general biography of Marcel Bliss or an unrelated Busga history. Bliss is a supporting lead only when it helps answer a Wittnauer question.

The main research question is:

> **How did Wittnauer obtain, adopt, modify, manufacture, document, sell, and eventually move away from the one-bezel `alarm setting + alarm winding` concept embodied by Cal.10WA?**

The `CH304088A / Marcel Bliss` route is therefore a means of investigating Wittnauer, not the endpoint of the project.

## Priority questions — Wittnauer side first

### 1. Wittnauer ↔ CH304088A bridge

Highest priority.

Look from the **Wittnauer / Longines-Wittnauer side** for evidence that can connect the production 10WA to `CH304088A` or to Marcel Bliss:

- patent assignment / licence records,
- Longines-Wittnauer patent files,
- Wittnauer technical bulletins,
- service sheets,
- parts catalogues,
- movement / module drawings,
- period trade notices,
- advertisements or catalogues mentioning patented construction,
- correspondence, inventor attribution, design credits,
- import / manufacturing / assembly records,
- legal or corporate records that name the technology source.

`CH304088A` itself is a strong structural match, but until a bridge is found do not write `CH304088A is the Wittnauer 10WA patent`.

### 2. Why Wittnauer used this concept

Try to establish the contemporary product logic rather than infer it only from later operation.

Questions:

- Did Wittnauer advertise `set + wind` as convenience, novelty, safety, elegance, reduced controls, or another benefit?
- Was the overhanging bezel / recessed crown / case architecture explicitly justified as part of that operating concept?
- Was the module sold as a patented feature?
- Was there a user instruction explaining the winding limit or a special operating sequence?

Best sources: period advertising, user manuals, dealer material, trade press, service literature.

### 3. Patent concept vs production implementation

Compare the patent concept with the surviving production 10WA without assuming they are legally the same invention.

Current high-value contrast:

- `CH304088A` explicitly recognizes the coupling problem created by combined `set + wind` operation.
- The patent describes a slipping-bride option that would preserve setting freedom at full wind.
- Horlbeck describes production 10WA behavior in which the bezel stops around full alarm wind and forcing it can damage transmission teeth.

Open questions:

- Does the 10WA alarm barrel use a fixed bridle, slipping bridle, or another arrangement?
- If it differs from the patent option, what exact production part / architecture causes the limitation?
- Is there a service instruction or parts drawing that explains the stop / transmission path?

Do **not** infer why Wittnauer used a different implementation until direct evidence appears. Cost, packaging, reliability, patent scope, supplier constraints, and production simplicity are hypotheses only.

### 4. Base movement — exact Wittnauer evidence

The objective is not to win `Longines vs AS1200` by web majority. The objective is to find a Wittnauer-era bridge source.

Current conflict:

- Horlbeck / Beitl: Longines-base.
- AWI / parts references: repeated AS1200-family compatibility with Wittnauer 10WA.
- FHF: AS1200 conclusion, but the posted `A SCHILD 1200 / WITT 11P` identification is itself in conflict with Marshall / Bestfit references that map `11P` to Peseux 170 and `11H` to AS1200.

Target evidence:

- Wittnauer parts catalogue,
- Wittnauer service sheet,
- Bestfit / Marshall / Paulson period catalogue page that directly names 10WA,
- factory ebauche stamp / plate comparison,
- contemporary movement identification table.

Until then preserve the conflict.

### 5. Why Wittnauer moved away from 10WA

Separate **sequence** from **cause**.

Already supported:

- Wittnauer later used AS alarm movements such as AS 1475 / 1568 / 1931 / 5008.

Still unproven:

- that 10WA was abandoned specifically because of fragility, service difficulty, user confusion, cost, sourcing, or production complexity.

Deep-dive factors to investigate:

- warranty / repair burden,
- dealer or watchmaker complaints,
- service complexity,
- production cost,
- supplier availability,
- reliability reputation,
- market acceptance,
- emergence of standardized AS alarm calibres,
- change in case / control ergonomics,
- corporate / procurement decisions inside Longines-Wittnauer.

Do not convert a plausible factor into a causal claim without a source.

## Source hierarchy for this research

Use sources in this order when they conflict:

1. **Wittnauer / Longines-Wittnauer primary material** — service sheets, parts books, manuals, ads, catalogues, corporate / legal records, patents assigned to the company.
2. **Direct patent / legal records** — e.g. `CH304088A`, assignment registers, family records, official gazettes.
3. **Surviving 10WA evidence** — owned specimen, teardown photos, movement markings, operating measurements, original box / guarantee / booklet.
4. **Period watchmaker / interchange literature** — Bestfit, Marshall, Paulson, AWI, trade bulletins.
5. **Specialist books** — Horlbeck, Beitl.
6. **Repair / watchmaker reports and specialist web** — useful for mechanism and failure evidence, but distinguish single-watch experience from product-wide claims.
7. **FHF research note / X posts** — valuable as a research path and clue source; independently verify technical claims and bibliographic references.
8. **General media / sales pages / forums** — discovery only unless independently supported.

## Current source roles

### `CH304088A`

Role: primary technical comparator.

Supports:

- dial-side add-on alarm module,
- independent alarm barrel,
- rotating bezel with internal toothed ring,
- combined alarm winding + alarm-time setting,
- stated user benefit of eliminating a separate alarm-winding task,
- recognition of full-wind setting conflict and a slipping-bride workaround.

Does not yet support:

- Wittnauer ownership,
- Wittnauer licence,
- direct identity with Cal.10WA,
- reason for production implementation choices.

### FHF (@FHF_Ebauche), 2024-08-09 / 10

Role: research-path / clue source.

Important contribution:

- patent-first route: unusual patent → search for surviving watch → 10WA,
- explicit AS1200 hypothesis,
- posted movement-identification image requiring bibliographic identification.

Do not use as sole proof of patent identity or base calibre.

### Horlbeck / Beitl

Role: specialist synthesis and technical / historical comparison.

High-value points:

- genuine modular architecture,
- Longines-base description,
- dedicated alarm barrel,
- bezel set + winding,
- production operating limitation / damage risk,
- later Wittnauer movement history.

Keep their Longines-base statement visible even when parts evidence points toward AS1200-family compatibility.

### Bestfit / Marshall / Paulson / AWI

Role: period / technical movement-identification and parts-interchange evidence.

Use them to establish:

- house-calibre equivalence,
- parts compatibility,
- movement-family relationships.

Do not automatically equate a shared interchangeable part with exact base-calibre identity.

### Repair / teardown records

Role: production-behavior and service-risk evidence.

Use for:

- broken transmission teeth,
- unusual disassembly sequence,
- observed module layout,
- operating constraints.

Do not generalize single repairs into `all 10WA are fragile`.

## Deep-dive causal model

When evaluating a claimed explanation for 10WA, separate these layers:

1. **Design goal** — what problem was the concept trying to solve?
2. **Mechanism** — how did the bezel / module / barrel achieve it?
3. **Trade-off** — what new constraint did the integration introduce?
4. **Production choice** — how did Wittnauer implement the concept in the actual watch?
5. **User consequence** — what operating sequence or risk resulted?
6. **Service consequence** — what failure / repair burden appears in surviving examples?
7. **Commercial consequence** — did this affect sales, warranty, cost, or supplier choice?
8. **Successor decision** — why did later Wittnauer alarms use other movement families?

A source may support one layer without supporting the next. Do not collapse the chain into a single causal story.

## Editorial implication

The research should retain respect for the engineering intent.

The current strongest narrative is not `a foolish design created a trap`, but:

> A one-control concept deliberately removed a separate user task. The underlying patent literature even recognized the resulting coupling problem and proposed a remedy. The production 10WA nevertheless exhibits a real full-wind setting constraint. The unresolved historical question is how and why Wittnauer arrived at that production implementation.

This direction is intended to support a later Deep Dive and catch-copy decision, not to force a catch before the evidence is mature.
