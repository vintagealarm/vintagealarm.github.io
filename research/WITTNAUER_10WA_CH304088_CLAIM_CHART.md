## Claim-text discipline

Separate three layers:

1. **CLAIM / SUB-CLAIM** — expressly included in the patent’s claim set.
2. **DESCRIPTION / EXAMPLE** — described in the specification or illustrated embodiment, but not necessarily independently claimed.
3. **PRODUCTION CORRESPONDENCE** — whether a surviving 10WA appears to implement the feature.

A feature being claimed does **not** prove Wittnauer used the patent. A feature matching 10WA does **not** prove licence, assignment, supply, or technology transfer.

## Important correction: slipping bridle

The slipping-bride feature is **not merely an incidental narrative suggestion** in the checked patent publication. It appears in **sub-claim 6** dependent on the bezel-winding + alarm-setting combination.

Therefore the safe interpretation is:

> CH304088A specifically claims, in one dependent implementation, a slipping-bride alarm barrel intended to preserve setting freedom at full wind. Surviving 10WA behavior does not show that freedom, but the exact production barrel / spring attachment has not yet been directly documented.

Do **not** write:

- `The patent required a slipping bridle in every implementation.`
- `Wittnauer removed the slipping bridle.`
- `The production 10WA definitely uses a fixed bridle.`

The claim hierarchy matters: the main invention can exist without every dependent sub-claim being present.

## Description vs claimed elements

The specification also explains the user benefit of the combined bezel operation: the wearer need not separately attend to winding the alarm spring because winding occurs when the alarm time is set.

This is valuable as **design-goal evidence inside the patent document**. It is not, by itself, evidence of Wittnauer’s marketing language or internal adoption motive.

## Three live relationship hypotheses

Keep all three alive until bridge evidence eliminates them.

### A — licence / acquisition / authorized use

Marcel Bliss’s concept or rights reached Wittnauer / Longines-Wittnauer, and the production 10WA was modified for manufacture.

Would be strengthened by:

- assignment / licence record,
- patent notice in Wittnauer material,
- correspondence / inventor credit,
- supplier agreement,
- technical documentation explicitly linking the patent / inventor to 10WA.

### B — technical contact without proven patent-rights chain

Wittnauer had access to the concept, supplier, designer, prototype, or know-how, but the legal chain is different, undocumented, or indirect.

Would be strengthened by:

- common supplier / workshop evidence,
- trade correspondence,
- drawings or parts that reproduce distinctive features without assignment evidence,
- inventor / supplier connection to a Wittnauer manufacturing source.

### C — independent convergence

Bliss and Wittnauer independently solved the same alarm-watch problem in very similar ways.

Would be strengthened by:

- no bridge after comprehensive chain-of-title / trade-source research,
- Wittnauer documents showing independent internal development,
- material differences in implementation chronology or design origin.

Current rule:

> Structural similarity can make A or B worth pursuing, but it cannot by itself eliminate C.
