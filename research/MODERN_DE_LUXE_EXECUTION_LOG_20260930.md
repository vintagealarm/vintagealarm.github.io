## 3. New clock chronology result — Fukuoka Kyosairen commemorative Modern De Luxe

Yahoo Auctions search currently exposes an object titled:

`置き時計 MODERN DE LUXE ALARM 創業20周年 1兆円達成記念 農協の共済 贈 福岡県共済連 ...`

Source:
https://auctions.yahoo.co.jp/search/search/1%E5%85%86%E5%86%86/0/

The marketplace title alone is not accepted as a date. The anniversary was independently checked against institutional bibliographic records:

- Fukuoka Prefectural Library lists `福岡県農協共済二十年史`, edited/published by `福岡県共済農業協同組合連合会`, publication `1975.6`.
- Fukuoka City Library independently lists the same title and issuing organization, `1975.6`.
- CiNii Books records the same title, issuing body and publication year `1975`, with holdings at 12 libraries and chronology on pp.121–147.

Sources:
- https://www.lib.pref.fukuoka.jp/iLisvirtual/?count=7&holcd=1103020491&key=01&keycode=1&type=2
- https://www.toshokan.city.fukuoka.lg.jp/iLisvirtual/?count=7&holcd=1005250574&key=01&keycode=1&mode=sp&type=2
- https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BN13861123

A separate Fukuoka library record also confirms `福岡県農協共済十年史` issued by the same organization in 1966, consistent with an institutional anniversary chronology but not used to invent an exact founding day.

Source:
https://www.lib.pref.fukuoka.jp/iLisvirtual/?count=7&holcd=1108198047&key=01&keycode=1&type=2

### Judgment

G4 `CLOCK → DATE` now **PASSES for a bounded 1975-era commemorative-event anchor**. The evidence supports that the object's stated `創業20周年` commemoration corresponds to the organization's 1975 twenty-year milestone.

It does **not** establish:
- the clock's exact manufacture date;
- that the clock was manufactured in 1975 rather than ordered shortly before/after;
- who supplied the clock;
- Hirota/H.M.C./Modern Royal involvement;
- any pre-1971 Modern De Luxe clock date.

This is stronger than seller-style decade dating because the commemorative inscription is externally anchored by the commemorating organization's own 1975 twenty-year history.
