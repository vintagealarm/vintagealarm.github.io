# Modern De Luxe — Council V3 Jester strategy reset

Date: 2026-10-03 JST  
Scope: PR #135 research branch only. No public-site change.  
Origin: Council V3 format 7 / 宮廷道化師, then explicitly adopted by the user.

## 1. Why this reset exists

The research had correctly separated movement maker, case maker, brand operator, exporter/distributor, and promotional-goods supplier. However, the actual search effort had become disproportionately centered on Hirota / H.M.C. / Modernlite and related lighter history while the primary clock-side bridge remained unresolved.

The critical untested premise was earlier than the Hirota question:

> Are the `Modern De Luxe` clock/watch, travel-clock, and lighter examples actually one brand / one operator family, rather than independent uses of the same product name?

Until that premise is tested, evidence from lighter-side `MODERN`, `H.M.C.`, `Modernlite`, Hirota, or Modern Royal must not be imported into the clock-side attribution merely because the names and market context look compatible.

This is a research-strategy decision, not new historical evidence.

## 2. New gate: G0 SAME BRAND / SAME OPERATOR

### Question

Do the `Modern De Luxe` clock/watch and non-clock `Modern De Luxe` products belong to the same brand/operator chain?

### PASS

At least one attributable source must connect the clock/watch side and another product category through a common commercial fingerprint, for example:

- same named company / operator
- same address
- same box / guarantee / instruction family
- same contemporaneous catalogue
- same trademark registration / owner
- same distinctive logo or crest **plus** an independent company/operator link
- same model or article-number system with a named operator
- contemporaneous source explicitly spanning both categories

Name identity alone is insufficient.

### Current

**OPEN.**

### Operational consequence

G0 is **not required** if G1 is solved directly from a clock/watch source.  
G0 **is required before lighter/H.M.C./Hirota evidence can be used to support clock-side attribution.**

## 3. Search-priority reset

### ACTIVE — highest information gain

1. **User-held Modern De Luxe specimen comparison, when physical evidence is available.**
   - compare the two specimens systematically rather than assume one production family
   - record all safe-to-observe markings, rear-control topology, movement architecture, case construction, dimensions, dial/case relationships, and any manufacturer or service marks
   - do not infer a maker from resemblance alone
   - do not require destructive or unsafe disassembly; use only safely obtainable evidence

2. **Exact Modern De Luxe clock/watch responsibility evidence.**
   - original box
   - guarantee / warranty
   - instructions
   - period advertisement
   - wholesale/export label
   - retailer/distributor label
   - service/warranty entity
   - catalogue entry naming an operator

3. **Exact-brand G0 evidence.**
   - seek sources where `Modern De Luxe` itself crosses product categories with a common company/operator fingerprint
   - exact-name evidence outranks generic `MODERN` evidence

4. **Customer reverse path for commemorative clocks.**
   - Fukuoka mutual-aid 20th-anniversary clock
   - Mitsubishi Electric Nagoya Works commemorative clock
   - seek procurement, vendor, supplier, corporate-gift, anniversary, or internal purchasing records

5. **Trademark body text — narrowed first to exact brand.**
   - first search `Modern De Luxe` / `Modern du Luxe` and distinctive logo/crest variants
   - only expand to broad `MODERN`, H.M.C., Hirota, or Ichikawa when the exact-brand result gives a reason to do so

6. **Movement / finished-clock maker only after the commercial/brand chain is materially narrowed.**

## 4. PARKED / CONDITIONAL

The following are no longer active default search branches:

- generic H.M.C. company-identity hunting
- generic Hirota corporate-history expansion

- additional Modernlite article-number accumulation
- generic `Modern ...` lighter collection

These branches may resume only when:

1. an exact `Modern De Luxe` source points into that branch; or
2. a direct clock/watch source creates a specific bridge question that the branch can answer.

Existing evidence is retained. It is not deleted or downgraded merely because the search priority changes.

## 5. Reinterpretation of existing evidence

### Modern Feudor / Hirota

Retain as strong evidence that Hirota manufactured/exported a lighter carrying the `Modern Feudor` designation in the 1960s.

Do **not** use it as proof that Hirota owned a broad MODERN brand family or operated `Modern De Luxe`.

The licensing/manufacturing context also supports an important alternative: Hirota could manufacture products whose brand/operator was elsewhere.

### H.M.C. / Modernlite

Retain the physical/product corpus as real.

Do **not** treat resolving `H.M.C.CO.` as a prerequisite to solving the Modern De Luxe clock. G3 becomes conditional unless a G0/G1 source creates a direct bridge to H.M.C.

### Modern Royal / Ichikawa

Retain the official 1971 joint-venture history and later lighter/watch-category evidence.

Do not project the 1971 Modern Royal structure backward onto earlier `Modern De Luxe` products without a contemporaneous bridge.

### 1975 Fukuoka event anchor

Retain G4 bounded PASS.

It dates the commemorative event/provenance context, not the manufacture date of the whole Modern De Luxe family and not the 55 mm wrist-alarm line.

## 7. Completion logic after reset

### COMPLETE-A

Unchanged in substance: at least one attributable source directly connects a Modern De Luxe clock/watch to a named commercial actor.

G0 may additionally establish whether the lighter/non-clock corpus belongs to the same operator family, but G0 is not necessary if G1 is directly solved.

### COMPLETE-B

Do not require endless completion of unrelated H.M.C./Hirota branches.

Accessible evidence may be considered exhausted when the **clock-side direct routes**, **exact-brand G0/G2 routes**, and **promotional/customer G5 routes** have reached their documented stopping points without a clock-to-company bridge.

If no exact Modern De Luxe evidence ever points to H.M.C., unresolved H.M.C. identity does not by itself prevent COMPLETE-B.
