## What is established strongly enough to retain

### Hirota / Royal / Modern Royal background

- Hirota is a real Japanese lighter-manufacturing/export actor; do not waste runs re-proving the generic fact that Hirota made lighters.
- Modern Royal must **not** be flattened into “another name for Hirota.” Official Modern Royal history supports the sequence: Kaname Ichikawa founded Ichikawa Sangyo in 1946; Royal Sangyo was established in 1965; in 1971 it combined with Hirota Modern Trading and was renamed Modern Royal. Later ownership/business transfers do not retroactively make all earlier MODERN goods Modern Royal products.
- U.S. design/patent material shows continued Ichikawa-side design activity after the 1971 combination. Keep Hirota-side and Ichikawa/Royal-side roles separable.

### Modern / H.M.C. physical product corpus

H.M.C. is not merely a seller-invented string and no longer looks unique to a single Art.510 item. Current corpus includes at least the following leads/products:

Research implication: it is now reasonable to prioritize H.M.C. as a recurring manufacturer/exporter/brand-management/product-line marking hypothesis rather than an Art.510-only marking. It remains **unknown what H.M.C. expands to or legally represents**.

### Modern De Luxe product / promotional pattern

- Modern De Luxe survives on clocks and on lighter-related goods. Do not infer common manufacturer merely from shared branding.
- Corporate/commemorative Modern De Luxe clocks are important because they support a promotional-goods/distribution route. Fukuoka mutual-aid and Mitsubishi Nagoya Works examples are leads/evidence in the ledger; do not convert commemorative-event dates into manufacture dates without a source.
- The current working model is that Modern De Luxe **may** have been a brand/distribution/promotional-goods line that could source clocks externally while Hirota manufactured lighter products. This model fits several fragments but remains a **working hypothesis**, not a finding.
