## Q1 — What is the base movement of Cal.10WA?

### New high-value evidence: 1966 Marshall service-parts table

A searchable scan of the **C. & E. Marshall Handy Manual (1966)** contains a Wittnauer parts table in which the `10WA` timekeeping side is listed with:

- time mainspring: `WIT3245`
- alarm mainspring: `WIT5102`
- balance staff: `WIT679`
- stem: `WIT158L`
- roller: `WIT89`
- pallet arbor: `WIT573`

The same Marshall table gives **Wittnauer 10S** the same timekeeping-side service identifiers: `WIT3245`, `WIT679`, `WIT158L`, `WIT89`, `WIT573`. The 10WA row differs by adding the separate alarm mainspring `WIT5102`.

Sources:
- https://device.report/m/a60e99145e5effac4489519a85b9e47f21dce1f47e4885f4c75f3995bb8795d5.pdf
- https://www.phfactor.net/wtf/Marshall%20Handy%20Manual/
- independent 1966 identification for the manual: https://www.ebay.com/itm/397550314877

Classification: `STRONG — near-period technical-service evidence`.

### Direct bridge: AWI 1969 identifies Wittnauer 10S as AS 1200

The American Watchmakers Institute technical bulletin from October 1969 states that amplitude tests were run on six watches, **all Wittnauer 10S (AS 1200)**.

Source:
- https://www.awci.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/10-1969-AWI-News.pdf

Classification: `ADOPTED — period watchmaker-institute identification of 10S = AS1200`.

### Combined inference

The chain is now substantially stronger than generic interchangeability:

1. 1966 Marshall gives 10WA and 10S the same listed timekeeping-side mainspring, balance staff, stem, roller and pallet arbor; 10WA separately adds an alarm mainspring.
2. 1969 AWI explicitly identifies Wittnauer 10S as AS1200.
3. Additional Bestfit-derived sources independently place 10WA with AS1200-family material for setting bridge, balance-cap screw and winding stem.

This strongly favors an **AS1200 / Wittnauer-10S-family timekeeping base** for production 10WA.

However, neither Marshall nor AWI literally says `10WA = AS1200`, so the evidence is still a technical bridge rather than a direct Wittnauer factory declaration.

### Remaining conflicting sources

Horlbeck explicitly writes that 10WA is based on a hand-wound Longines calibre. Beitl likewise labels it `Longines-Basiskaliber` and describes a 1956 Longines-signed 10WA.

Classification update for article purposes:

> `STRONG / PROVISIONAL — AS1200/10S-family base favored by near-period technical-service evidence; later Longines-base attribution remains a documented conflict.`

Do **not** present the dispute as 50/50 anymore, but do not claim absolute factory-level resolution until a Wittnauer / Longines-Wittnauer factory sheet directly names the base.

## Q3 — Did production 10WA implement the slipping-bride solution?

CH304088A explicitly describes and sub-claims a `ressort à bride glissante` solution that preserves alarm-setting freedom even when the alarm spring is fully wound.

Source:
- https://patents.google.com/patent/CH304088A/en

Production evidence instead shows a hard full-wind limit: Horlbeck states that at about 1.5 turns the bezel blocks and forcing it can tear teeth from the transmission wheel.

The 1966 Marshall table adds one useful production fact: 10WA has a separate alarm mainspring service number `WIT5102`, distinct from the time mainspring `WIT3245`.

A detailed 10WA teardown confirms the dial-side alarm module and alarm spring / gear train, but the accessible teardown does not expose or identify the inner barrel-to-spring attachment sufficiently to determine fixed vs slipping bridle.

Source:
- https://watch.weblog.to/archives/49044123.html

The surviving-parts listing identifies the complete alarm barrel as `7182 / X3154`, but does not show the internal attachment.

Source:
- https://www.ebay.com/str/brian727272store

Classification: `OPEN — functional divergence established; exact production bridle construction still unverified`.

Scope decision: do not pursue the full X31xx family. Resume Q3 only for an X3154 barrel interior, factory/service drawing, or direct component-level evidence capable of identifying the mainspring attachment.
