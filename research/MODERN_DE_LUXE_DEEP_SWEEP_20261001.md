## 1. 1961 JPO locator remains unpromoted

The 2021 r/translator thread states that the poster had obtained a Japan Patent Office document for a lighter. A translation comment reads the document as:

- `東京都台東区浅草橋3-19` / Taito-ku, Asakusabashi 3-19;
- Showa 36 (1961), requested and issued;
- owner/name `広田良夫`;
- no company name given.

Source locator:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/translator/comments/mi6492

The underlying JPO image/gazette number was not recovered in this pass. Therefore none of the above is promoted to independently verified JPO fact; it remains a locator transcription.

## 2. Official Hirota chronology gives a stronger address constraint

Lightec's official corporate history states:

- 1937: founded in Sakamoto 1-chome, Taito;
- 1938: moved to Asakusa Senzoku-cho;
- **1946: moved to the present location in Asakusabashi, Taito**;
- 1947: reorganized as Kabushiki Kaisha Hirota Seisakusho;
- 1954: reorganized as Kabushiki Kaisha Hirota;
- 1964: a reinforced-concrete headquarters building was completed in Asakusabashi.

Source:
- https://lightec-inc.jp/corporate/history.html

Current Lightec/parent-company records give the present address as `2-13-9 Asakusabashi, Taito-ku, Tokyo`.

Control source:
- https://www.wesleynet.com/thailand/ecatalogue/profile.php?Co_Code=1105

### Consequence

If the official phrase `現在地` is literal and historically continuous, then a 1961 record reading `浅草橋3-19` is **not a clean address match to the corporate Hirota headquarters**. This materially weakens any shortcut of the form `1961 広田良夫 + 浅草橋 = 株式会社広田`.

This does not disprove a relationship. Possible explanations include a private residence, a separate office/workshop, old addressing/renumbering, transcription loss (e.g. omitted sub-number), or an unrelated Hirota. None is currently demonstrated.

## 3. The `3-19` string is especially unsafe without the original image

Modern address search shows `浅草橋3-19-11` as a real address (Tobita Building). This is not evidence about 1961 Hirota; it demonstrates that a transcription such as `3-19` may be incomplete at sub-number level and should not be normalized to the current Hirota address by intuition.

Example modern control:
- https://www.hl-pro.com/about-us

No evidence was found in this pass that the current/official Hirota address `2-13-9` was formerly numbered `3-19`, or that `3-19` was a Hirota corporate site.

## 4. Later Yoshio Hirota remains a separate confirmed node

USPTO-derived records independently show `Yoshio Hirota (Tokyo)` as inventor of cigarette-lighter designs filed in 1984–1985, assigned to `Kabushiki Kaisha Hirota`. Example D284410.

Source:
- https://patents.justia.com/patent/D284410

This confirms a later Yoshio Hirota directly tied to the Hirota company and lighter design. It still does not identify the 1961 `広田良夫` as the same person.

## 6. New research consequence: `広苑 第3号` rises to top-tier primary target

A 1966 copy of Hirota's internal magazine `広苑 第3号` is independently catalogued by a Japanese antiquarian bookseller as:

- publisher/context: 株式会社広田 (now Lightec) internal magazine;
- 1966;
- B5, 56 pages;
- description explicitly mentions **`浅草橋のライター製造業`**.

Source:
- https://www.kosho.or.jp/search/%E8%A3%BD%E9%80%A0%E6%A5%AD?pageno=4

This is now more valuable than further generic web searching because it is period, company-internal, geographically exact, and explicitly about the Asakusabashi lighter industry. It could contain contemporaneous addresses, company/person names, suppliers, competitors, photographs, advertisements, or terminology capable of testing the 1961 `3-19` locator and H.M.C. identity.

The contents have **not** been read in this pass. No claim is made that H.M.C., MODERN, Modernlite, or Yoshio Hirota appears inside.

## Bottom line

Instead it found a substantive contradiction/constraint: official Hirota history says the firm moved to its `present location` in 1946, while the current address is 2-13-9 Asakusabashi, not the locator's 3-19. The research therefore becomes stricter: recover the original JPO record and period corporate address evidence before using the 1961 lead.
