Source:
- https://www.ebth.com/items/14093726-jimmy-schnozzola-durante-s-rare-vintage-longines-wittnauer-alarm-watch

Classification: `ADOPTED — documented surviving provenance/date inscription / HOLD — factory production date`.

What it supports:

- this physical Ref.1216 / Cal.10WA example existed in gift circulation no later than **1959-02-10** if the inscription provenance is genuine;
- it is a useful terminus ante quem for this surviving example and a dated U.S.-market anchor.

What it does not support:

- exact manufacturing date;
- exact retail-sale date;
- first or last production date of Ref.1216;
- a factory chronology for all 10WA watches.

The inscription date must not be silently converted into a production date.

### A15. The repair-risk evidence has now been independently observed on another surviving 10WA, but causation remains unproven

A 2026 Uhrforum post documents a 10WA whose alarm-module gear teeth had been broken after apparent improper operation. The owner / repair context specifically warns that over-rotating the bezel can damage the alarm mechanism.

Source:
- https://uhrforum.de/threads/black-beauty-vintage-wittnauer-10wa-alarm-watch-mit-aussergewoehnlichem-zifferblatt-und-tonfeder.624189/

This converges with Horlbeck's production-operating warning and with the Japanese overhaul report showing the bezel-to-alarm winding transmission.

Classification: `ADOPTED — recurring surviving-watch failure mode / HOLD — population-wide fragility / OPEN — commercial consequence`.

Safe statement:

> Forcing the bezel after the alarm winding system reaches its limit can damage the transmission teeth, and this failure mode is documented in more than one surviving-watch / specialist source.

Unsafe statement:

- `all 10WA are fragile`;
- `this flaw caused Wittnauer to discontinue 10WA`;
- `warranty burden forced the switch to AS`.

Those causal / prevalence claims still require period service, warranty or company evidence.

## Track B — patent / CH304088A connection history

Google Patents records `CH332208A`, titled `Marquise watch`, with:

- original assignee: `Bliss Marcel`
- filing / priority date: `1957-12-09`
- publication: `1958-08-31`
- subject: a wristwatch / bracelet-case construction using spring-hinged arched branches.

Source:
- https://patents.google.com/patent/CH332208A/en

Classification: `ADOPTED — repeated watch-patent activity under the same inventor name / OPEN — personal identity bridge`.

What it supports:

- `Bliss Marcel` is not visible in the checked patent index only once at CH304088A; the same name appears as inventor / original assignee on another watch-specific Swiss patent five years later.

What it does not yet support:

- that this is legally / biographically proven to be the same Marcel Bliss later associated with Montres Busga;
- any Wittnauer employment, licence, consulting relationship or assignment;
- any connection between CH332208A and 10WA technology.

The value of CH332208A is as an identity / activity lead, not as another 10WA patent.

### B11. A Busga / Marcel Bliss identity hypothesis is now concrete enough to investigate, but remains secondary-source HOLD

Grail Watch Wiki's Busga corporate history states that:

- Montres Busga was controlled by Fritz and Emma Bliss by 1952;
- `Marcel Bliss of Zurich` received power of attorney in 1959;
- Busga introduced the `Capitol Alarm Timer` at the 1958 Basel Fair;
- Marcel Bliss later entered the board / presidency succession.

Source:
- https://wiki.grail-watch.com/index.php/Busga

Classification: `HOLD — identity / industry-context hypothesis`.

Why it matters:

- CH304088A was filed in 1952 under the name Bliss Marcel;
- CH332208A was filed in 1957 under the same name;
- secondary corporate history places a Marcel Bliss of Zurich in a Swiss watch manufacturer by 1959, and that firm had introduced an alarm product in 1958.

This is a much more targeted lead than a generic Bliss biography.

But the bridge is not proven. Do not write:

- `the CH304088A inventor was Busga's Marcel Bliss`;
- `Busga supplied the 10WA module`;
- `Wittnauer licensed Busga technology`.

Required bridge evidence: patent application address / representative, commercial-register identity, correspondence, assignment / licence record, or another primary source tying the patent applicant to the Busga individual.

### B12. Busga itself was patent-active in watches during the same period, but this still does not bridge CH304088A

This establishes a period fact worth pursuing: Busga itself was filing Swiss watch patents between the two checked `Bliss Marcel` filings.

Classification: `HOLD / contextual patent activity` until the original CH323379A record and inventor / representative details are directly inspected.

Research use:

- if names / addresses / representatives converge, the Busga identity hypothesis strengthens;

### B13. CH304088A remains a technically strong match and still provides no indexed Wittnauer name

Source:

Classification remains: `STRONG structural correspondence / OPEN legal-commercial bridge`.

No change to the A/B/C relationship hypotheses.
