# VINTAGE ALARM

Mechanical alarm wristwatch history, specimens, sound and sources.

Canonical website: https://vintagealarm.github.io/

## Research & external-reference principle

VINTAGE ALARM does **not** treat external outreach as SEO sales or reciprocal-link building.

1. **No SEO-sales / reciprocal-link outreach.** Backlinks, rankings, or AI citations are not the goal by themselves.
2. **Contribute first.** When approaching an external database, wiki, publication, or researcher, provide useful sources, data, technical material, or owner/specimen observations before asking for any reference to VINTAGE ALARM.
3. **Expand routes to the evidence.** The goal is that someone who did not already know the watch can still reach the information they need—especially the existence of real specimens, operation, alarm sound, mechanism, and differences between sources—regardless of which search engine, database, wiki, article, research publication, or AI surface they start from.

Detailed policy: `strategy/external-reference-outreach.md`

## Editorial durability

VINTAGE ALARM favors a small number of durable, source-traceable WATCH pages over database-scale content growth. Multilingual factual revisions, targeted SPEC exceptions, reproduced-primary-source labeling, correction intake, AI/SEO guardrails, uneven article depth, and long-term evidence-media preservation are governed by:
