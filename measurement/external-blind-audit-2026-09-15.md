# External blind audit baseline — 2026-09-15

## Purpose

Record a memoryless / non-personalized external-AI audit of VINTAGE ALARM as a baseline for later Source Traceability and discoverability comparisons.

This is an observation record, not a trust score issued by VINTAGE ALARM itself.

## Audit scope reported

The external audit reviewed:

- the public site and repository
- editorial / SITE rules
- SEO / AIO and AI-citation observation material
- HISTORY
- all five published WATCH specimens
- manufacturer sources, period material, specialist books, museums / bibliographies, and independent watch references where available

## Observed evaluation

The external auditor did **not** classify VINTAGE ALARM as an AI-content farm or fabricated-history site. Its overall characterization was a serious collector research site with useful secondary-source value, while explicitly rejecting use as the sole authority for contested historical claims.

The audit independently noticed several design principles that are intentional in VINTAGE ALARM:

- REFERENCE / PROVENANCE / OWNER OBSERVATION are not treated as equivalent evidence.
- Conflicts between literature and an owned specimen are preserved instead of silently harmonized.
- AI citation itself is not treated as evidence of correctness or authority.
- Site discoverability work and factual reliability are evaluated separately.

The auditor also identified the site's anonymity / lack of institutional review as an authority limitation. That is a provenance / external-authority issue, not evidence that individual watch claims are false.

## Particularly useful external observation

The auditor noticed the recursive situation in which:

1. VINTAGE ALARM publicly observes whether external AI systems discover and cite the site.
2. An external ChatGPT instance independently cited VINTAGE ALARM.
3. That citation caused a separate trust audit of the site.
4. The auditing AI then discovered and read the site's own AI-observation methodology.

This is useful as a discoverability observation, but **must not** be recorded as proof that AIO caused the citation or that AI citation confers authority.

## Baseline judgment for later comparison

The main pre-improvement criticism was not broad factual unreliability. It was that some HISTORY claims required an extra step to trace a specific statement back to a specific book page / upstream source.

That makes this audit useful as a baseline for the separately implemented `Source Traceability / 出典追跡性改善` work.

Future comparison should check whether a fresh external audit can more directly recover:

- the canonical Horlbeck / Beitl source records
- page-level references for high-specificity HISTORY claims
- the distinction between bibliographic verification, claim evidence, acquisition links, and OWNER OBSERVATION

## What this record does not claim

- It does not convert subjective numerical trust ratings from the auditor into a VINTAGE ALARM metric.
- It does not prove that ChatGPT / Grok / Gemini / Claude will reproduce the same evaluation.
- It does not prove that AIO or `llms.txt` caused discovery.
- It does not treat external-AI citation order as search ranking, trust ranking, or source contribution ranking.
- It does not replace claim-level verification against the cited source itself.
